# DATA DICTIONARY: features_daily.csv (S&P 500 realized-volatility project, free supplementary data)

Built 2026-10-07 (ET) on the box from public, keyless endpoints. Rows: every NYSE trading day from 1990-01-02 to 2026-10-06 (9,258 rows; 2026-10-06 is the last completed session with settled data from all daily sources). Index column: `date` (YYYY-MM-DD, the NYSE session date).

## Alignment rules

- **Daily series** are aligned by **observation date** (the value for calendar date t goes on NYSE day t). They are **not forward-filled**: NaN means the source has no value for that date (bond holiday, HK holiday, before the series starts, after its latest release). Values on weekends and NYSE holidays (EPU, ADS, news sentiment, GPR) are dropped. They are not averaged into Monday.
- **Weekly series** (NFCI, STLFSI4) are aligned by a **conservative release date** (week-ending Friday + 6 calendar days) and carried forward until the next release. Nothing is filled before the first release.
- **Real-time ADS** (`ads_index_realtime_*`) comes from the Philly Fed vintage file: on day t, the newest observation in the latest vintage released strictly before t.
- Observation-date alignment of a lagged or revised series is fine for in-sample replication (Kilic 2025 and Christensen et al. use final-vintage data). For a genuine out-of-sample forecast, shift columns by the recommended lag below.

## Release lags and look-ahead summary

| Series | Typical availability of value for day t | Revised? | Suggested shift for real-time use |
|---|---|---|---|
| S&P 500, HSI, VVIX/VXN/VXD/RVX (market closes) | end of day t (HSI before the NYSE open) | no | 0 (features at t for target t+1..) |
| T10Y3M, DGS10 | evening of t | rarely | 0 |
| HY OAS (BAMLH0A0HYM2) | t+1 on FRED | occasionally | 1 day |
| EPU daily | morning of t+1 | slightly | 1 trading day |
| ECB New CISS (US, EA) | about t+1 business day | yes, recent weeks | 1-2 trading days; tail revised |
| SF Fed news sentiment | weekly update, up to about 7 days lag | occasionally | about 5 trading days for strict real-time |
| AI-GPR | about monthly update | yes (method updates) | up to about 1 month for strict real-time; in-sample use as is |
| GPRD classic | about weekly | occasionally | about 5 trading days |
| ADS (current vintage) | about weekly, about 5 days lag | HEAVILY | use ads_index_realtime_latest instead |
| NFCI, STLFSI4 | Wed/Thu after week end | yes (NFCI full history re-estimated) | already release-aligned |
| Fama-French factors | monthly, about 1 month lag | occasionally | cannot be real-time daily; in-sample only, or lag about 1-2 months |

## Mapping to papers

- **Kilic (2025)**: log VIX and ΔT-bill 3M (from your existing VIX and Treasury data; not in this file), 10Y-3M slope (`t10y3m_spread_pctpts`), ADS (`ads_index_current_vintage` / `ads_index_realtime_latest`), EPU (`epu_daily_index`), SF Fed news sentiment (`sf_news_sentiment_index`), CISS (`ciss_us_new_index`; `ciss_ea_new_index` as an alternative, so check which region the paper uses), AI-GPR (`ai_gpr_index`), FF5 (`ff_mkt_rf_pct`, `ff_smb_pct`, `ff_hml_pct`, `ff_rmw_pct`, `ff_cma_pct`).
- **Christensen et al.**: ADS, ΔT-bill (existing data), EPU, Hang Seng squared return (`hsi_sq_logret_pct2`), VIX (existing data).
- **Extras**: VVIX, VXN, VXD, RVX, HY OAS (last 3 years only), NFCI, STLFSI4, DGS10, FF momentum, GPR sub-indices, S&P 500 OHLC and range-based variances (Parkinson; Garman-Klass where the open is not stale).
